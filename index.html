Geometria da Informação de Transição

Transition Information Geometry

A physics derived from a single premise, with no adjustable input.

Rui Pedro Rodrigues Salomé  ·  Independent researcher, Portugal
ORCID 0009-0006-9892-9601

The framework begins from one premise — distinction, that one state differ from another — and asks what follows if nothing else is put in by hand.

From that single starting point it derives a chain of structures: a complex amplitude, a resonant cavity, a spectrum of stopping points, and from these the particle content, the mixing angles, the gauge group, and a reading of several cosmological quantities. The construction carries no adjustable parameter; it rests on one dimensionless constant, itself an output rather than an input.

Every quantity is traceable to the construction, and every claim is marked for what it is: a recovery of something already known, or a prediction the construction risks. Where a result requires an external ruler, that is stated rather than hidden.

The papers below are the work as it stands, in reading order. The first is the root; each that follows leans on the ones before it. They are offered openly — to read, to check, to refute.

These are preprints, deposited on Zenodo with a public timestamp; none has yet been through journal peer review. What is claimed for them is coherence and verifiability, not endorsement. The reader is invited to judge.
