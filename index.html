The Unwinding Spiral

A parameter-free informational root for the Standard Model, gravity, and cosmology

Rui Pedro Rodrigues Salomé · ORCID 0009-0006-9892-9601

This is an open collection of papers developing a single construction: a two-dimensional informational grain that unwinds along a logarithmic spiral and is projected into 3+1 physics by one fixed step. From two integers, with no fitted parameter, the construction returns the gauge group of the Standard Model, three colours, three generations, the electric charges, the charged-lepton mass ratios, and the leptonic mixing angles; and at the other end of the same spectrum, the dark-energy equation of state, flat galaxy rotation curves without dark matter, and colour confinement as an exact prohibition.

The aim is not familiarity but reproducibility. Every number quoted is computed by a self-contained program that takes the two integers and prints each result beside its measured value; it runs in under a minute and needs only numpy and sympy. Where a result is recovered from established work rather than derived here, it is marked as such and the priority is named.

These are preprints, deposited on Zenodo with DOIs and dates; they have not been peer-reviewed. What is claimed for them is internal coherence and external verifiability, not the authority of a journal. The reader is invited to run the program and check.
