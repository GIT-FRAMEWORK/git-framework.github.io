# Transition Information Geometry (GIT)

**A physics derived from a single premise, with no adjustable input.**

Rui Pedro Rodrigues Salomé · Independent researcher, Portugal
ORCID: [0009-0006-9892-9601](https://orcid.org/0009-0006-9892-9601)

---

Transition Information Geometry (GIT — *Geometria da Informação de Transição*)
begins from one premise — **distinction**, that one state differ from another —
and asks what follows if nothing else is put in by hand.

From that single starting point it derives a chain of structures: a complex
amplitude, a resonant cavity, a spectrum of stopping points, and from these the
particle content, the mixing angles, the gauge group, and a reading of several
cosmological quantities. The construction carries **no adjustable parameter**;
it rests on one dimensionless constant, itself an output rather than an input.

Every quantity is traceable to the construction, and every claim is marked for
what it is: a **recovery** of something already known, or a **prediction** the
construction risks. Where a result requires an external ruler, that is stated
rather than hidden.
